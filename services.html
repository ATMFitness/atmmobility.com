Services at ATM

A practical place to begin.

Choose focused training, hands-on therapeutic care, or an adaptive approach that makes room for the person in front of us. Appointments are available at our North York centre, with eligible GTA home visits for selected services.

Personal Training

Build a routine around how you move now.

One-to-one coaching starts with your goals, current movement, and the kind of support that fits your life. Sessions can focus on strength, mobility, conditioning, or a return to a regular practice without promising a one-size-fits-all result.

Boxercise brings boxing-inspired movement into a coached session. Group Training offers a shared format for friends, families, or small teams while keeping the plan connected to the people taking part.

What to expect

We begin with a conversation about where you are starting and what you would like to work toward. From there, the session can be adjusted as your needs and schedule change. Personal Training is available at the centre or at home through eligible GTA visits; a travel fee applies to home visits.

Pricing is customized. Contact us to discuss one-to-one, Boxercise, or Group Training.
