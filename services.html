Services at ATM

Training and therapeutic care for the way you want to move.

Build strength, improve mobility, make room for recovery, or explore a more individualized way to train. ATM brings coaching and hands-on care together in North York, with select services also available through eligible GTA home visits.

Personal Training

Build strength with coaching that has a clear purpose.

One-to-one training is shaped around what you want to improve—strength, conditioning, mobility, confidence, or getting back into a consistent routine.

Choose individual coaching, Boxercise for boxing-inspired conditioning, or Group Training for friends, families, and small teams.

Pricing is customized by format.

Adaptive Training

Training that adapts to the person.

Adaptive Training is an individualized, sensory-aware approach to movement for children and adults. Anthony works with people whose needs may not fit a conventional training environment, including people living with chronic illness.
