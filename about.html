About ATM

A place to move, recover, and work at your own pace.

ATM Mobility & Therapy brings personal training, adaptive movement, and hands-on therapeutic care together in a North York centre where the plan can stay personal.

Our approach

Start with the person, then shape the session.

Goals matter, but so does the way someone arrives that day. ATM sessions are built around a clear starting point, useful conversation, and enough flexibility to adjust the pace, setting, or structure when needed.

The centre is deliberately straightforward: room to train, room for hands-on care, and a team of independent practitioners bringing different specialties under one roof.

The centre

Two practical spaces, one address.

ATM Mobility & Therapy operates at 542 Champagne Dr., North York, ON. The centre includes a training studio for strength, conditioning, boxing-inspired work, and mat-based movement, plus a private treatment room for massage, FST, and other bodywork.

Parking is available on site. For directions, appointment setting, and home-visit information, see Visit & Contact.

Plan your visit

Centre or home visit

Selected services are also available through eligible GTA home visits. Travel fees apply, and the scheduler determines availability.

For practitioners

Space for your practice.

Independent practitioners can request use of ATM's private treatment room or training studio. Tell us your practice type, the space you need, and your preferred schedule so the request can be reviewed.

A request is not an instant confirmation. If you want to ask about fit before requesting space, contact ATM directly.
