Adaptive Training

Movement at a pace that feels right.

A sensory-aware approach to movement for young people ages 8–25. We make room to get comfortable, understand what matters, and build a session around the individual.

Before the first session

Understanding needs comes first.

The free consultation is a chance to talk about goals, preferences, sensory considerations, and the setting that may feel most workable. It helps us begin with a shared understanding instead of assumptions.

Sessions are available at the North York centre or through eligible GTA home visits. A travel fee applies to home visits, and the scheduler confirms availability.

For ages 8–25

Adaptive Training is designed for young people who may benefit from a patient, sensory-aware approach to movement.

In the session

Structure that can change with the day.

We adapt the session structure to the person: the pace, the environment, the kind of instruction, and the amount of new material. There is time to pause, reset, or try a different route when that is what the moment calls for.

The aim is not to rush toward a fixed outcome. We move at the client's pace and keep the next step clear enough to feel manageable.
