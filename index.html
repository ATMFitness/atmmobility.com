The ATM approach

Individual attention, without the pressure.

Every session begins with what matters to you: a goal, a concern, a return to routine, or simply a need to feel more at home in your body. We listen, work at a useful pace, and adapt the plan as you go.

There is no one prescribed route through the work. The aim is a practical session that respects your starting point and leaves room for your changing needs.
